📝 About Me

👋 Hello, World!

I'm Reynaldi Hadianto, a Senior Consultant at Cevo in Sydney, NSW. I have over 15 years of experience in enterprise cloud migration and quality engineering. I started my career in test automation, and I now lead end-to-end AWS migrations for large organisations.

My work removes manual toil from the software delivery lifecycle. I automate infrastructure provisioning, I secure container runtimes, and I build CI/CD pipelines that teams trust. On migration programs I deliver the technical outcome, and I also coach customer teams in agile practice and AWS skills.

I hold the AWS Solutions Architect Associate and AWS Cloud Practitioner certifications. I am extending that base further, and I explore how artificial intelligence and new cloud services help build scalable and secure platforms.

🎯 I have worked in both small and large teams, in roles that include technical architect, business analyst, project manager, test lead, developer and operations.
